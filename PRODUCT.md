# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
German vocational schools (for Ausbildung placement), prospective companies/employers, technical managers, and open-source collaborators. They are looking for clear proof of high technical competence, systems/database level understanding, and structural software engineering discipline.

## Product Purpose
To serve as anesdevv's elite professional storefront, highlighting database design skills, concurrent scripting capability, and rich mobile development (Flutter) to secure premium Ausbildung opportunities and developer engagements in Germany.

## Brand Personality
Sleek, high-performance, technical, reliable, minimalist dark-tech.

## Anti-references
Generic light SaaS clichés, excessively rounded cards, flat/washed-out grey layouts, side-stripe borders, or superficial marketing buzzwords.

## Design Principles
1. Technical Precision: Leverage grid alignments, monospace accents, and developer terminal aesthetics to showcase hard skills.
2. High-Contrast Contrast: Use a locked dark slate background paired with striking emerald/teal highlights for optimal reading and visual luxury.
3. Authentic Demonstration: Prefer real repository logs, direct source links, and responsive layouts over mock text or abstract stats.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Full WCAG 2.1 AA text contrast compliance, support for screen readers via proper semantic markup, high-contrast layouts, and strict `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` styling checks.
